# ArtSource

Where the game's art and sound are made and kept, outside Unity. Each asset has one folder with
its `brief.md` (the brief and the ChatGPT prompt), an `AI/` folder (raw output in `_raw/`, plus
the cleaned draft), and a `Hand/` folder for the redraw.

The rules are in `docs/art/PROVENANCE.md`; what exists and what is left to redraw is in
`docs/art/MANIFEST.csv`.
