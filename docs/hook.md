The one line hook would be a Castle Crashers-style co-op combat with Skyrim/Dungeon Defenders-style gear progression and an endless loot chase. The core vision is a game where players generally move from left to right with lmited vertical movement. Basic combat is simple and responsive. Characters should tie into the story and have a small number of abilities such as elemental attacks, movement abilites, and specials, and even though they will differ visually, they work mechanically similar (like how each character has different visual magic in castle crashers but they are all pretty similar mechanically). Different characters will use mechanically similar abilites with different animations, elements, effects with small gameplay differences (fire will work better in cold environments, like how dungeon defenders has ehas enemies with elemental resistances.)  Players collect equipment with different tiers, qualities, and possible addons/enchants. Lower-tier equipment has lower maximum potentials, while rarer equipment may have better stats and offer more min/max customization. The goal is to make finding gear exciting without making combat complicated.
