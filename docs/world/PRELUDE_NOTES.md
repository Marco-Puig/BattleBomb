## 1. Intro / Prelude

**Opening crawl**
- Sets up like a Shrek "far, far away" fantasy land: mythical creatures, vibrant forests and varied
  biomes, and a central fantasy kingdom.
- The kingdom is surrounded by villages that often need help.
- The Knight Squad is sent out from the main kingdom to help villagers.

## 2. Prelude: Tutorial (slime attack)

*(Slimes don't have to be slime — any enemy that fits works.)*

- A village on the outskirts of the kingdom is being attacked by slimes.
- A carriage pulls into the village and the player character(s) jump out.
- This fight is the tutorial: tooltips for the basics — movement, attacking, picking up loot, other
  foundational controls.
- The village is saved once all slimes are cleared.
- Slimes here are generic / gray (important contrast for later).

**Return to the castle**
- The player (or players, in co-op) report back to the main kingdom.
- The king is very pleased and is surrounded by his Knight Squad, who always guard him.
- Everyone is celebrating another victorious day in the castle.

**The inciting event**
- Everything starts shaking: a huge earthquake and a loud noise.
- Something has fallen out of the sky.
- Everyone is confused; the player gets ready to investigate.
- Before they can go, an alien breaks through the castle's front door / wall.

**King vs. alien**
- Everyone gets ready to fight, but the king steps up to fight the alien himself, saying the
  kingdom is his responsibility.
- The king rips off his royal clothing and is revealed to be super buff, wielding a gold legendary
  sword.
- The king and the alien take their fight outside the castle.

**Minion fight**
- Before the player can follow, they must fight minions the alien spawned inside the castle.

**Aftermath / hook into Chapter 1**
- After beating the minions, the player goes outside.
- The king and the alien are gone.
- All that's left is a large crash-landed alien spaceship.
- Slimes are outside again, but mutated: much larger than the prelude slimes, with magical glowing
  colours — red, purple, blue (vs. gray before).
- Fighting these slimes leads into Chapter 1.
- The player realises the alien has "amped up" the fantasy monsters.

## What carries through the game

- The player doesn't know what has happened to the king — **who won the fight** is the question
  they carry while they play and progress.
- Shape of the whole game: the player goes exploring, collecting gear, learning, and levelling
  (Dungeon Defenders-style grind) for **a big fight at the end** (like Castle Crashers).
