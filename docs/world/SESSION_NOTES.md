## Q1 — The goal (2026-09-26, Michael after talking it over with Marco)

*Asked: when the heroes leave the castle for Chapter 1, what are they setting out to do?*

> Our original duties as low level knights has been to defend the castle and the nearby village
> from the regular monsters that are not a big deal, so we must go carry out our regular duties to
> defend. In doing so we pass by the alien ship, we learn they are after some sort of artifact. We
> clear out the new amped up enemies within the village and we now know the aliens give buffs to
> the regular monsters, we must venture out to figure out what the aliens want... (I don't have
> anything planned beyond this point) we fight regular monsters that haven't received the buff,
> more as in learning what they are capable of and how to fight them, and as we get closer to mini
> bosses we start finding monsters with the glowing alien aura. We continue on, eventually we find
> the artifact and also the king badly injured by the big alien boss... we continue on some more
