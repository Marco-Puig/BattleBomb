# Draft 1 — The Heartstone Hunt

**Pitch:** the king led the alien away from the castle on purpose. He is the decoy. The knights
follow the trail of the duel across the land, and it leads to the thing the alien actually came
for.

**Tone:** an earnest hero story that is not afraid of a joke. The king is a legend and everybody
knew it but us. The aliens are cold and strange. The land is bright and full of life worth saving.

**Working names:** the kingdom is Bramblehold. The alien is the Harvester. The artifact is the
Heartstone.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is the Heartstone: a fist-sized stone that is the source of all the land's
  magic. Every monster, every element, every enchanted sword exists because of it. The king's line
  hid it in a shrine far from the castle generations ago, and only the king knows where.
- **Why the aliens want it:** they harvest worlds. A Heartstone is a world's magic in one piece.
  Their touch leaks that same kind of power, which is why the monsters they pass turn huge and glow.
- **The king** knows the alien came for him, because he knows the way. So he takes the fight
  outside and *keeps going*, dragging the alien across the land, away from the castle. He is
  found badly hurt at the shrine's door, having failed to stop it there. He lives.
- **Leaves the land?** Only for the finale: the mothership comes down to collect.
- **Final boss:** the Harvester, grown enormous on the Heartstone's power, aboard its own ship.
- **Endless:** the Heartstone came back cracked. It leaks. The crash site outside the castle
  never stops spawning amped monsters, and the Knight Squad rotates through it forever.

---

## The arc at a glance

1. **Prelude** — the tutorial village, the celebration, the crash, the king's reveal, the minion
   fight. Outside: the ship, the mutated slimes, and a trail of destruction leading away.
2. **Chapter 1: the Outskirts** — follow the trail of the duel through the farmland and forest.
   Learn what the ship was after. Boss at the trail's end. The trail continues into the mountains.
3. **Chapter 2: the Frostpeaks** — the trail climbs. The land itself is being drained: things
   freeze and go grey where the alien walked. Boss. From the summit, see where the trail goes.
4. **Chapter 3: the Sunken Marsh** — the shrine is in here. Find the king. Find the shrine open
   and empty. Overhead: the mothership descending.
5. **Chapter 4: the Mothership** — climb aboard. Take it back.
6. **After** — the crash site as Endless.

## Prelude

As written in `../PRELUDE_NOTES.md`. This draft's specifics:

- **The crawl** says: a far, far away land; a kingdom ringed by villages; the Knight Squad rides
  out whenever a village calls; you are its newest members.
- **Inside the crashed ship** (seen through the hole the crash tore in it, a set piece the player

  other worlds. One empty jar sits ready, bigger than the rest. Speeding through, you see a
  collector's shelf with a gap in it.
- **The trail:** from the castle gate, a line of destruction runs off screen right. Torn ground,

## Chapter 1 — The Outskirts

**Region:** farmland into forest, the villages the knights have always protected. Bright,
vibrant, slightly wrecked. **Climate:** neutral (nothing boosted or weakened): it is the teaching
chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** the first village from the tutorial, now with a crater in the square
and its gray slimes turned huge and glowing. The trail of the duel cuts straight through it.

**The road:** the knights do their job (clear the villages) and the job leads them along the
trail. The enemy family is the land's ordinary creatures: slimes as grunts, a thrown-spore
mushroom folk as ranged, a will-o'-wisp as caster, and a boar-thing as brute. Early stages use the
plain versions. Nearer the boss, the glowing ones appear.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the trail crosses a field where the ground is grey and dead in a wide
circle, plants withered, colour gone. The alien stood here. Everything it touches, it drains.
Paying attention: the alien takes life from the land. Speeding through: bad thing kills grass.

**The boss:** the Hollow Oak, the forest's oldest tree, drained and hollowed by the alien's
touch, walking. It slams its trunk down along the lane (jump it) and sweeps a root across the
whole width of the screen at one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a sword, *Heartwood*.

**Handoff:** the Oak falls across a ravine and becomes the bridge. On the far side the trail
climbs into mountains, and the peaks in the distance have gone grey and frozen in a band, like a
stripe of death across the range.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Frostpeaks

**Region:** mountains, snow, ice caves, a mining town. **Climate:** Ice strengthened, Fire
weakened for enemies and players alike. The Fire knight's first hard chapter; the Ice knight's
first great one.

**What you see on arrival:** the grey stripe from below is a path of frozen ground, exactly the
alien's footsteps, wide as the duel. Trees snapped. A crater where the king clearly threw the
alien into a cliff.

**The road:** mountain creatures: ice wolves as grunts, spear-throwing yeti-folk as ranged, frost
sprites as casters, a stone-shelled ram as brute. The glowing ones now come with the frozen
ground, so the player learns to read the alien's trail as danger.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the mining town, half of it drained grey and frozen. In the square, the
king's gold sword is planted in the ground. He has gone on without it. Walking past, the player
sees it is too big for anyone else to lift. Paying attention: the king was hurt here and left his
weapon. Speeding through: the king's sword is here, so he was here.

**The boss:** the Glacier Wyrm, a serpent of ice woken by the draining, amped and glowing. It
dives under the snow and erupts beneath the player (jump the eruption) and breathes a beam of frost
down one lane (change lane). **Drop:** a chestpiece, *Wyrmscale*.

**Handoff:** from the summit the whole land is visible. The grey stripe runs down the far side of
the mountain into a marsh, where one bright light is shining from the middle of the dark water.
The trail and the light are the same place.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Sunken Marsh

**Region:** wetlands, drowned ruins, a shrine on stilts. **Climate:** Earth strengthened, Air
weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the grey stripe enters the water and the water itself has gone dead
and still along it. The light ahead is the shrine.

**The road:** marsh creatures: frog-folk as grunts, blowpipe lizards as ranged, bog witches as
casters, a mud golem as brute. The amped ones are everywhere now; the alien slowed down here,
fighting the king the whole way.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king. Found on the shrine steps, cape gone, armour cracked, sitting
against a pillar. Alive. As the player approaches he points up the steps and stays where he is.
Speeding through: the king is hurt and pointing that way.

**The boss:** the Shrine Warden, a giant stone guardian the king's ancestors built, corrupted by
the alien's touch and turned on the shrine it guards. It stomps along the lane (jump the
shockwave) and sweeps its staff across the width of the screen at one depth (change lane).
**Drop:** a helmet, *Warden's Crown*.

**Handoff:** the shrine door hangs open. Inside, on a pedestal, the shape of a stone in dust. It is
gone. The shrine goes dark. Then the marsh goes dark, because something enormous has come between
the land and the sun: the mothership, descending, a beam of light dropping from it to a hilltop
where the Harvester waits with the Heartstone glowing in its grip.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Mothership

**Region:** the beam carries the player up. Inside the ship: alien halls, jar-rooms full of other
worlds' trophies, a throne room. **Climate:** Fire strengthened (the ship runs hot), Ice
weakened, so the Fire knight gets the finale.

**The road:** the alien's own minions from the prelude, now in every archetype, and every jarred
trophy from the ship's walls set loose on the player as the ship wakes up.

**The final boss:** the Harvester, grown huge on the Heartstone's power, the stone burning in its
chest. It stamps the floor and a ring of energy runs out along the ground (jump it), and it drags
a beam of drained grey light down one lane (change lane). Halfway through, it drains the arena
itself grey and the fight moves to the throne. **Drop:** the Heartstone itself is story, not gear;
the gear drop is a pet, *Harvester's Eye*, a floating eye that hovers by the player.

**The ending:** the Heartstone falls out of it and rolls to the player's feet. Pick it up with
Light, the same as any drop. The ship shudders, the beam opens, and the player is carried back
down to the hilltop as the ship rises and leaves.

---

## After

The results screen. Then the castle: the king back on the throne, bandaged, the gold sword returned
to his side. The Heartstone on a plinth in the throne room, glowing, a visible crack down its
face.

**Endless:** the crack leaks. The crash site outside the castle, where the first ship still lies,
never stops spawning amped monsters, and the Knight Squad rotates through it in shifts. That is
Endless mode: the knights' new standing duty, from the castle gate, forever.

---

## Speeding through

The village, the castle, the crash, the buff king, the fight outside. You follow the wreckage the
fight left. Everywhere the alien walked, the land dies. You find the king's sword. You find the
king. You find an empty shelf, and the alien standing on a hill holding the thing that was on it,
and a giant ship coming to pick it up. You go up, you beat it, you bring the thing home.
