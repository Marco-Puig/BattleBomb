# Draft 2 — Follow the Crown

**Pitch:** the king's crown is what keeps the land's monsters tame. The alien collects crowns. In
the duel the crown comes off, and it spends the rest of the game being passed from monster to
monster like a hot potato while everything that touches it grows huge. You chase the crown.

**Tone:** silly. Shrek by way of Castle Crashers. The king is a lovable buff idiot. The alien is a
fussy collector who is terribly annoyed by everything. Monsters are goofy. Nothing is scary,
everything is a bit ridiculous, and the jokes are visual because nobody speaks.

**Working names:** the kingdom is Muchgood. The king is King Barnaby the Reasonable. The alien is
the Curator. The artifact is the Crown.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is the Crown. It is enchanted: whoever wears it, the land's creatures obey. That
  is why the kingdom's monsters have always been "not a big deal", and why the Knight Squad's job
  was easy. The king never mentioned this because the king is not a details person.
- **Why the aliens want it:** the Curator collects crowns. It has a ship full of them, each one
  the rule of a world, on little velvet cushions. It wants this one for the shelf. It amps up
  monsters because its touch is wrong for this world; things swell and glow near it, which it finds
  distasteful.
- **The king** takes the fight outside, and the crown flies off his head in the second swing. It
  lands on a slime. The slime grows to the size of a house and hops away. The king and the
  Curator both chase it, and so does the player. The king is found later, badly hurt, having tried
  to take the crown back from something much too large. He lives, and he is furious about it.
- **Leaves the land?** No.
- **Final boss:** the Curator, finally wearing the Crown, now king of every monster in the land,
  grown enormous, at the castle it wants to redecorate.
- **Endless:** the Curator's collection ship, still crashed outside the castle, is full of every
  other world's crowned monsters, and the jars did not survive the crash. The Knight Squad clears
  the hold, shelf by shelf, forever.

---

## The arc at a glance

1. **Prelude** — village, celebration, crash, the king's reveal. Outside: the crown on a giant
   glowing slime, bouncing away. Follow the slime.
2. **Chapter 1: the Outskirts** — chase the crowned slime through the villages. Boss: the crowned
   slime. The crown lands on something else and flies off.
3. **Chapter 2: the Highlands** — chase a crowned bird thing into the peaks. Find the king. Boss.
   The crown falls into the sea.
4. **Chapter 3: the Sunless Shore** — chase the crown along the coast and under it. Boss. The
   Curator finally catches it and heads for the castle.
5. **Chapter 4: Home** — back through the land, now obeying the Curator, to the castle.
6. **After** — the collection ship as Endless.

## Prelude

As written in `../PRELUDE_NOTES.md`. This draft's specifics:

- **The crawl** is short and cheerful: a land far, far away, with a very good king, a very nice
  castle, and monsters that are honestly not much trouble. You are the newest, smallest knights.
- **The king's reveal** stays exactly as Michael described: royal robes torn off, super buff, gold
  legendary sword. This draft adds the Crown staying on through it, slightly too small for his
  head, which is the setup.
- **Inside the crashed ship** (seen through a torn wall as the player passes): a display case
  with a dozen crowns on cushions, each strange, each from somewhere else. One cushion is empty
  and labelled. Speeding through: it collects crowns; there's a gap.
- **Outside:** the king and the Curator are gone. The mutated slimes are here. One of them is the
  size of a house, glowing, and has the Crown on its head, and it is bouncing away over the hill
  with a trail of smaller glowing slimes behind it. Everyone follows the crown.

## Chapter 1 — The Outskirts

**Region:** farmland, orchards, three villages. **Climate:** neutral: the teaching chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** the crowned slime's trail: a line of enormous round dents in the
ground, hedges flattened, a windmill wearing a slime.

**The road:** the land's ordinary creatures, but now out of control because the Crown is not on
the king: slimes as grunts, pumpkin-headed scarecrows as ranged, garden gnomes as casters, a
runaway bull as brute. The player learns that "not a big deal" is over.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king, running through the background of a village, chasing the
slime, sword raised, still in his torn robes. He does not stop. Speeding through: the king is
chasing the slime too, and he is fine.

**The boss:** the Crowned Slime, a house-sized glowing slime with a crown. It bounces along the
lane and lands (jump the splash) and rolls sideways across every depth at once except the one it
started from (change lane to where it was). **Drop:** a pair of boots, *Slimestompers*.

**Handoff:** the slime pops. The Crown goes flying, spinning, and lands on a passing crow. The crow
grows to the size of a horse, glows, and flies off toward the mountains, screaming. The king runs
after it through the background.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Highlands

**Region:** hills into mountains, a monastery, cliffs. **Climate:** Air strengthened, Earth
weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** giant crow feathers, glowing, scattered along the path up. Every
cliff-top creature is bigger than it should be, because the crown flew over it.

**The road:** highland creatures: goat-folk as grunts, rock-throwing hill trolls as ranged, wind
spirits as casters, a rolling stone-ball beetle as brute.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king. In the monastery courtyard, flat on his back, enormous
crow-footprints around him, a monk waving a fan at him. He sits up as the player arrives, points
at the sky, and lies back down. Speeding through: the king is hurt, the bird did it, the bird went
that way.

**The boss:** the Crowned Crow, a huge glowing bird with a crown, nesting on the monastery
tower. It dives along the lane (jump it as it passes low) and beats its wings to sweep a gust down
one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a bow, *Longfeather*.

**Handoff:** the crow drops the Crown. It tumbles off the cliff, bounces down the rocks, and
splashes into the sea far below. The Curator is standing on the beach, sees it, and stamps its
foot. The player climbs down.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Sunless Shore

**Region:** coast, tide caves, a drowned village under the cliffs. **Climate:** Ice strengthened,
Fire weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the tide going out along the shore in a glowing line. Something under
the water has the Crown, and everything in the water near it is swelling up.

**The road:** shore creatures: crab-folk as grunts, squid-ink spitters as ranged, jellyfish
lanterns as casters, a shell-backed hermit thing as brute. The Curator is visible in the
background through the chapter, wading, poking the water with a stick, being unhappy.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the Curator gets pinched by a crowned crab and hurls it into the sea.
Speeding through: the alien is chasing the crown too, and it is losing.

**The boss:** the Crowned Kraken, in the flooded cave, wearing the crown on one eye-stalk. Tentacles
slam down along the lane (jump them) and drag across a single depth (change lane). **Drop:** a
piece of equipment, *Ink Sac*, an active that drops a blinding cloud.

**Handoff:** the Kraken lets go of the Crown. It rolls across the cave floor to the player's feet.
The Curator's hand comes down from off-screen and takes it. The Curator puts it on. It grows tall,
the glow spreads from it out through every monster in sight, and every monster in sight turns and
kneels. It walks off toward the castle with a parade of monsters behind it.

---

## Chapter 4 — Home

**Region:** back through the land to the castle. Familiar stages, wrecked, every creature marching
the same way as the player. **Climate:** neutral again, so the finale is fair to all four
knights.

**The road:** everything from every chapter, obeying the Curator now, and glowing. The player
fights up the road behind the parade and through the castle's broken wall from the prelude.

**The final boss:** the Curator, giant, crowned, sitting on the king's throne, which is too small
for it. It summons kneeling monsters up as a wave along the floor (jump it), and swings its
collection case across one depth as a club (change lane). Late in the fight the throne room's
ceiling falls in, and the fight is under the open sky. **Drop:** a helmet, *The Second Crown*, a
crown-shaped helmet with the collection's look; the real Crown is story.

**The ending:** the Crown falls off. It rolls across the floor and stops against the boot of the
king, who has limped in through the broken wall on a crutch. He puts it on. Every monster in the
land sits down. The Curator, small again, is picked up by the scruff.

---

## After

The results screen. Then the throne room: the king crowned, crutch beside the throne, the Curator
in a jar on the shelf beside him.

**Endless:** the Curator's crashed ship is a hold full of other worlds' crowned monsters, and the
jars broke in the crash. The Knight Squad is sent in to clear it, one shelf at a time, and the
shelves go on further than anyone has counted. That is Endless mode.

---

## Speeding through

Village, castle, crash, buff king, fight outside. The king loses his hat to a slime. The hat makes
things huge. Chase the slime. Chase the bird. Chase it into the sea. The alien gets the hat,
becomes the boss of every monster, and walks home to your castle. Go home. Take the hat back. Give
it to the king.
