# Draft 3 — The Fallen Star

**Pitch:** two things fell from the sky that night: the ship, and the thing it was chasing. The
star-shard is the ship's own heart, torn loose on entry and shattered across the land. Every
creature that swallowed a piece is glowing. The alien needs every piece back. So does the king.

**Tone:** adventure and wonder, in between earnest and silly. The land is beautiful and full of
strange things; the star makes it stranger. The alien is desperate rather than evil, which does
not make it any less dangerous. The king is a big warm hero.

**Working names:** the kingdom is Everbright. The alien is the Pilot. The artifact is the Star.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is the Star: the ship's power core, a burning crystal. When the ship came down
  it broke apart on entry, the core tore loose, and it shattered. The largest piece struck far
  away; smaller shards fell all over the land. The ship crashed at the castle chasing the fall.
- **Why the aliens want it:** without the core the ship cannot leave, and the Pilot is stranded.
  With every piece back, it can leave. With every piece back *and* a world's worth of amped
  monsters in tow, it can do a great deal more than leave. The shards are what amp the monsters: a
  creature that swallows one grows huge and glows. The Pilot's own aura is the same light.
- **The king** takes the fight outside, sees the streak in the sky where the biggest piece went
  down, and goes after it, with the Pilot on his heels. He is found badly hurt at the edge of the
  great crater. He lives.
- **Leaves the land?** No, but the finale is on the ship as it lifts off, and it ends in the sky.
- **Final boss:** the Pilot, made whole, the core burning in its chest again, on the deck of the
  rising ship.
- **Endless:** the sky is still falling. Every so often a shower of shards comes down on the crash
  site, and the Knight Squad is sent to clear what they land on before it spreads. Star-fall
  nights are Endless mode.

---

## The arc at a glance

1. **Prelude** — village, celebration, crash, the king's reveal, minions. Outside: the ship with a
   hole where its heart was, glowing slimes, and a sky still streaked with falling light.
2. **Chapter 1: the Outskirts** — follow the smallest shards through the villages. Boss: the
   first thing that swallowed a big one. See the next streak.
3. **Chapter 2: the Shardwood** — a forest where a shard hit and the trees grew around it
   overnight. Boss. From the canopy, the great crater on the horizon.
4. **Chapter 3: the Crater** — the biggest piece. The king. The Pilot takes the piece and runs for
   the ship.
5. **Chapter 4: Liftoff** — race the Pilot back to the castle; fight on the deck as the ship rises.
6. **After** — star-fall nights as Endless.

## Prelude

As written in `../PRELUDE_NOTES.md`. This draft's specifics:

- **The crawl** says: a far, far away land under a very big sky; a kingdom ringed by villages;
  the Knight Squad rides out whenever a village calls; you are its newest knights. The last line:
  tonight, something is falling.
- **The earthquake** is the ship. But the loud noise before it, the one that shook the plates on
  the feast tables, was the Star breaking apart overhead. Through the great hall's windows: the sky
  full of falling sparks, like a slow firework.
- **The crashed ship** has a hole in its front, edges melted, exactly the shape of something that
  tore itself out. Speeding through: the ship is missing a piece.
- **Outside:** the king and the Pilot are gone. The glowing slimes are here, and every one of them
  has a spark of light in its belly. On the horizon, a streak of fire is still hanging in the sky,
  pointing to where the big piece landed.

## Chapter 1 — The Outskirts

**Region:** farmland, orchards, the villages the knights have always protected. **Climate:**
neutral: the teaching chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** small craters everywhere, each with a scorched ring, each with
something glowing hopping away from it. The shards are small here. The monsters are only a bit
bigger.

**The road:** the land's ordinary creatures with sparks in them: slimes as grunts, thrown-seed
dandelion folk as ranged, glow-worms as casters, a hedgehog the size of a cart as brute. Plain
ones early, shard-lit ones near the boss.

**The mid-chapter beat:** a village well glowing from inside. A shard fell into it. The player
passes it; behind them, something climbs out. Paying attention: the shards make things huge.
Speeding through: the glowing things are the problem, and they come from the sky.

**The boss:** the Star-Struck Ox, the village's plough ox, which ate the biggest shard in the
region, now the size of a barn and lit from within. It charges the length of the lane (jump over
its lowered head) and swings its horns across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a chestpiece,
*Oxhide*.

**Handoff:** the Ox falls. The shard rolls out of it, still burning, and the player can walk over
it: it shows an item card like any drop, but the Pilot's hand snatches it before the player can
grab it. The Pilot runs off screen right, into the treeline, where the forest is glowing.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Shardwood

**Region:** a forest that grew a hundred years in a night around a shard. Trees the size of
towers, glowing sap, paths that were roads yesterday. **Climate:** Earth strengthened, Fire
weakened, so the Earth knight has the chapter and the Fire knight has to think.

**What you see on arrival:** the treeline. Normal trees, then trees ten times too tall in a
circle, all leaning toward the middle, where the light is.

**The road:** forest creatures grown with the wood: beetle-folk as grunts, sap-spitting
tree-frogs as ranged, moss shamans as casters, a walking stump as brute. The Pilot is glimpsed in
the background twice, climbing toward the light.

**The mid-chapter beat:** a clearing where the Pilot is caught in a giant spiderweb, struggling. The
player passes it. Behind the player, it tears free. Speeding through: the alien is here and it is
having a bad time too.

**The boss:** the Heartwood Widow, a spider the size of a house, glowing, the region's shard in her
abdomen. She drops from the canopy along the lane (jump the landing) and spins a web across one
depth that sticks (change lane). **Drop:** a pet, *Little Widow*, a spider that follows the player.

**Handoff:** the Widow's shard is taken by the Pilot before the player can reach it, and it climbs
the tallest tree with it. The player follows up. From the canopy, the whole land: villages, forest,
and on the horizon a crater so big it has its own weather, a column of light rising out of it.
The Pilot glides off the tree toward it.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Crater

**Region:** the great impact site: a rim of thrown-up rock, a bowl of glass, the biggest piece of
the Star in the middle. **Climate:** Fire strengthened, Ice weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the rim, and down in the bowl a landscape of melted glass, everything
lit from below, and in the very centre the Star, half-buried, burning.

**The road:** glass creatures born of the impact: glass-shard golems as grunts, molten-drip
spitters as ranged, ember wisps as casters, a crawling lava-turtle as brute. Everything here is
amped.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king. On the rim's inner slope, sitting against a rock, armour
scorched, sword across his knees. Alive. He lifts the sword and points it down into the bowl, then
lets it drop. Speeding through: the king is hurt, and the fight is down there.

**The boss:** the Pilot's Champion, the largest thing the Star has made: a creature that was
something small, now a glowing glass giant, guarding the Star for the Pilot who is busy digging it
out in the background. It slams both fists along the lane (jump the shockwave) and breathes a
line of molten glass down one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a sword, *Starglass*.

**Handoff:** the Champion shatters. The Pilot has the Star free. It holds it up, and every shard it
has collected flies into it and the Star is whole. The Pilot's aura flares. It leaps, and it can
fly now, and it flies back the way the player came, toward the castle, toward its ship.

---

## Chapter 4 — Liftoff

**Region:** the road home at a run, then the ship. **Climate:** Air strengthened, Earth weakened,
so the Air knight gets the finale.

**The road:** the land between the crater and the castle, every creature the Pilot passed on the
way now lit and frenzied. Then the castle grounds, and the ship, its hole sealed, humming, its
ramp down.

**The final boss:** the Pilot, whole again, the Star in its chest, on the deck as the ship lifts.
The deck tilts. It stamps a ring of light across the deck (jump it), and fires a beam of the
Star's light down one depth (change lane). Halfway through, the ship breaks the clouds and the
fight is in the open sky. **Drop:** a piece of equipment, *Pilot's Wing*, an active that gives a
short glide.

**The ending:** the Star breaks out of its chest. The player picks it up with Light. The ship,
heartless again, falls. The player rides it down. It lands in the same crater it made in the
prelude.

---

## After

The results screen. Then the throne room: the king bandaged on his throne, and the Star, cracked
into a hundred pieces again, in a great glass jar beside him, still glowing.

**Endless:** the sky is not done falling. Every so often a shower of shards comes down on the
crash site, and what they land on grows. The Knight Squad is sent out on star-fall nights to clear
it before it spreads. That is Endless mode.

---

## Speeding through

Village, castle, crash, buff king, fight outside. Something is falling from the sky and it makes
monsters huge. The alien is picking up the pieces. Follow the glow. Beat the thing that ate the
piece. The alien takes it. Chase it to the big crater. Find the king hurt. The alien gets the last
piece and flies home to its ship. Get on the ship. Beat it in the sky. Bring the pieces home.
