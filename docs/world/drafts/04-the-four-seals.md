# Draft 4 — The Four Seals

**Pitch:** the land's magic comes from a Wellspring under the castle, held shut by four elemental
seals at four shrines: Fire, Ice, Earth, Air. The aliens want to open it and bottle the lot. Each
chapter is one seal, one climate, one knight's home ground. The story ends back under the castle.

**Tone:** earnest, big, elemental. The land is old and its magic is a real thing with rules. The
aliens are an industrial operation, not a villain with a face until the end. The king is the
hero's hero. Jokes come from the king and from the monsters, not from the plot.

**Working names:** the kingdom is Fourhold. The alien is the Foreman. The artifact is the
Wellspring.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is the Wellspring: the source of all magic in the land, a spring of raw power
  in a cavern under the castle. Long ago it flooded the land with monsters and wild magic until
  the first king sealed it with four seals, one per element, each kept at a shrine in the region
  that element rules. The four knights are, by tradition, one from each shrine's region. That is
  why they wield what they wield.
- **Why the aliens want it:** to bottle it. A world's magic is fuel to them. Their probe, the
  ship, crashed into the castle because that is where the Wellspring is; the earthquake was the
  ship's drill hitting the cavern roof. The Foreman's job is to break the four seals so the
  Wellspring can be drawn off. Its touch leaks raw magic, which is what amps the monsters.
- **The king** knows exactly what the alien is here for. He takes the fight outside and sets off
  for the shrines to hold the seals. The knights follow. He is found badly hurt at the third shrine,
  having held it as long as he could. He lives.
- **Leaves the land?** No. The finale is home, under the castle.
- **Final boss:** the Foreman, at the Wellspring, drinking from it.
- **Endless:** the Wellspring's seals are broken and remade, but not as well as the first king made
  them. It leaks. The land's monsters never fully calm again, and the Knight Squad's standing duty
  is the crash site over the cavern, where the leak is worst. That is Endless mode.

---

## The arc at a glance

1. **Prelude** — village, celebration, the drill-quake, the king's reveal, minions. Outside: the
   ship with a drill in the ground, glowing slimes, and four beams of light in four colours on the
   horizon: the shrines, lit in alarm.
2. **Chapter 1: the Emberfields (Fire)** — the nearest shrine. Boss. The seal breaks.
3. **Chapter 2: the Frostreach (Ice)** — the second shrine. Boss. The seal breaks.
4. **Chapter 3: the Deeproot (Earth)** — the third shrine. The king. Boss. The seal breaks.
5. **Chapter 4: the Skyreach (Air)** — the last shrine. Boss. The last seal breaks, and the whole
   land goes bright.
6. **Chapter 5: the Wellspring** — home. Under the castle. The Foreman.
7. **After** — the leak as Endless.

Early Access ships Chapters 1 and 2: the Fire and Ice shrines, which are the two elements whose
statuses already exist (Burn, Chill), and two climates that flip each other's strength.

## Prelude

As written in `../PRELUDE_NOTES.md`. This draft's specifics:

- **The crawl** says: a far, far away land, whose magic rises from a spring under the castle,
  sealed long ago by four shrines; a kingdom ringed by villages; the Knight Squad rides out
  whenever a village calls; you are its newest knights, one from each shrine.
- **The four heroes** are the four shrines' knights: the Fire knight from the Emberfields, the Ice
  knight from the Frostreach, the Earth knight from the Deeproot, the Air knight from the Skyreach.
  Names, looks and personalities are Michael and Marco's; this draft only gives each a home.
- **The earthquake** is the ship coming down and its drill going in. The great hall floor cracks in
  a line and glows faintly from underneath. Speeding through: the ground under the castle is
  lit.
- **The crashed ship** is half-buried nose-first with a drill bit in the earth, and pipes and
  tanks along its side, all empty. Speeding through: this is a machine for taking something.
- **Outside:** the king and the Foreman are gone. The glowing slimes are here. On the horizon,
  four beams of light in four colours: red, blue, green, white. The nearest, red, is closest.

## Chapter 1 — The Emberfields (Fire)

**Region:** volcanic farmland, black soil, lava creeks, the Fire shrine at a caldera's edge.
**Climate:** Fire strengthened, Ice weakened. The Fire knight's home; the Ice knight's hardest
chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** the red beam ahead, and under it the land is burning worse than it
should: the seal is being strained.

**The road:** ember creatures: cinder slimes as grunts, ash-spitting salamanders as ranged, flame
sprites as casters, a magma tortoise as brute. Plain versions early; aura-lit ones nearer the
shrine.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king, on a ridge in the background, fighting a crowd of aura-lit
monsters alone, winning, heading toward the beam ahead of the player. Speeding through: the king
is fine and going the same way.

**The boss:** the Ember Warden, the shrine's guardian, a great fire-bull of living stone the first
king set to keep the seal, turned by the Foreman's aura. It charges along the lane (jump its
lowered horns) and stamps a line of fire down one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a sword,
*Emberfang*, with a Fire infusion.

**Handoff:** the Warden falls. The seal, a great glowing sigil on the shrine floor, cracks and goes
dark. The red beam goes out. Far off, the blue one flares brighter. The Foreman's silhouette is
seen leaving the shrine's far door, toward it.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Frostreach (Ice)

**Region:** glacier, ice caves, a frozen harbour, the Ice shrine inside a berg. **Climate:** Ice
strengthened, Fire weakened. Everything Chapter 1 taught, reversed.

**What you see on arrival:** the blue beam, and under it the ice is spreading down the valley
faster than weather, swallowing the harbour.

**The road:** frost creatures: ice wolves as grunts, icicle-throwing yeti-folk as ranged, frost
witches as casters, a mammoth as brute.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the harbour: the Foreman's smaller ships (landers, the size of carts)
lined along the frozen quay, filling tanks from cracks in the ice that glow. The player breaks
through them. Speeding through: they are collecting something from the ground.

**The boss:** the Frost Warden, the shrine's guardian, a great wolf of ice, turned. It leaps the
length of the lane (jump its landing) and howls a cone of frost down one depth (change lane).
**Drop:** a chestpiece, *Wardenpelt*, with Ice resistance.

**Handoff:** the seal cracks. The blue beam goes out. The green one flares. The king's gold sword
is found planted at the shrine's far door, glowing faintly, too big to lift: he went on without
it. Speeding through: the king is ahead, and he is in trouble.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Deeproot (Earth)

**Region:** caverns, a mine, roots the size of halls, the Earth shrine in the deep. **Climate:**
Earth strengthened, Air weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the green beam coming *up* out of the ground through a cave mouth,
and the earth around it heaving.

**The road:** deep creatures: mole-folk as grunts, crystal-spitting lizards as ranged, root
shamans as casters, a stone golem as brute.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king. At the shrine's inner door, sitting against the seal itself,
badly hurt, one hand still on the sigil, which is cracked but glowing. He held it. As the player
approaches he takes his hand off it and points to the door beyond. Speeding through: the king held
the line here and now it's your turn.

**The boss:** the Root Warden, the shrine's guardian, a great stone bear, turned. It slams the
ground and a wave of rock rolls down the lane (jump it) and sweeps its claws across one depth
(change lane). **Drop:** a helmet, *Wardencrown*, with Earth's crit passive.

**Handoff:** the seal breaks. The green beam goes out. The white one flares, straight up, from the
mountain above. The cavern's roof splits and a stairway of roots leads up into the light.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Skyreach (Air)

**Region:** mountaintop, floating islands, the Air shrine on the highest one. **Climate:** Air
strengthened, Earth weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the white beam, and the islands around it being pulled upward and
apart.

**The road:** sky creatures: harpy-folk as grunts, wind-dart hawks as ranged, storm spirits as
casters, a cloud-ram as brute. The Foreman's landers are everywhere now, tanks full and glowing.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the landers lift off together, all of them, heading for the castle in
the distance. Speeding through: they've got what they came for and they're going home.

**The boss:** the Sky Warden, the shrine's guardian, a great eagle of wind, turned. It dives along
the lane (jump it) and beats a gust down one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a bow, *Wardenwing*,
with Air's knockback passive.

**Handoff:** the last seal breaks. The white beam goes out. And then the whole land lights up:
every stream, every tree, every stone glowing from within, because the Wellspring is open. The
light is brightest at the castle, where a column of it rises through the roof. The islands
descend, gently, and set the player down on the road home.

---

## Chapter 5 — The Wellspring

**Region:** the castle, through the great hall's split floor, down into the cavern. **Climate:**
neutral: the finale is fair to all four.

**The road:** the castle's own halls flooded with raw magic, every kind of monster from every
chapter spilling up out of the cavern, aura-lit. Then the cavern: the Foreman's drill from above,
its pipes, the Wellspring itself, a pool of light, and the four broken seals around it.

**The final boss:** the Foreman, at the pool, drinking from it, growing with every mouthful. It
stamps a ring of raw magic across the floor (jump it), and sprays a jet from the pool down one
depth (change lane). Each time it is hurt it goes back to the pool. Late in the fight the four
seals, one by one, flicker back on as the player stands on each. **Drop:** a pet, *Wellspring
Wisp*, a small light that follows.

**The ending:** the Foreman bursts. The drill shudders and withdraws up through the roof, and
the ship above it lifts and goes. The four seals glow steady. The Wellspring settles.

---

## After

The results screen. Then the throne room: the king bandaged on his throne, the crack in the floor
mended, glowing faintly, and the four knights each standing on their seal's colour.

**Endless:** the seals are remade, but not as well as the first king made them. The Wellspring
leaks. The land's monsters never fully calm, and the leak is worst at the crash site over the
cavern, where the drill went in. The Knight Squad holds it in shifts, forever. That is Endless
mode.

---

## Speeding through

Village, castle, crash, buff king, fight outside. There is a machine drilling into your castle.
Four coloured beams on the horizon; go to the nearest. Beat the big guardian; the beam goes out;
the next one lights. The king is ahead of you, then his sword, then him, hurt. Four beams out, and
the whole land lights up, brightest at home. Go home. Go down. Beat the thing drinking the light.
