# Draft 5 — The Vault Under the Mountain

**Pitch:** the alien beats the king and carries him off. It needs him alive: the artifact is locked
in an ancient vault under the mountain, and only a king of the blood can open the door. The
knights chase the alien across the land, watching the king get hauled away at the end of every
chapter, Castle Crashers style, and catch up at the vault door as it opens.

**Tone:** classic Castle Crashers. Medieval fantasy to the bone: castles, fens, mountains, a
dungeon at the end, a dragon. The alien is a thing that fell from the sky, and the land treats it
as a demon or a star-beast; nobody in the story has a word for "alien", and the story never needs
one. The king is a legend, and being carried around in a cage does not make him less of one.
Big, funny, heroic.

**Working names:** the kingdom is Oldmark. The alien is the Star-Beast. The artifact is the
Dragon.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is a dragon, the last one, asleep for a thousand years in a vault under the
  mountain. The first king of the land fought it, could not kill it, and sealed it in with a door
  that only his blood can open. The kingdom was built on that story. The knights have heard it as a
  bedtime tale.
- **Why the aliens want it:** a dragon is a weapon. The Star-Beast crossed the sky for it, and the
  thing it found on landing was that the door will not open for it. It needs a king. So it takes
  one. Its aura amps whatever it passes, which is how it makes an escort out of the land's
  ordinary monsters as it goes.
- **The king** takes the fight outside and, for the first time in his life, loses. The
  Star-Beast throws him in a cage of its own light and carries him off toward the mountain. He is
  found badly hurt at the vault door, having been made to open it, and he lives. He is furious.
- **Leaves the land?** No.
- **Final boss:** the Star-Beast riding the Dragon, then the Star-Beast alone when the Dragon
  throws it.
- **Endless:** the vault under the mountain is not one room. Below the Dragon's chamber the first
  king's dungeon goes down further than anyone has mapped, and everything down there woke up when
  the door opened. The Knight Squad goes down, level by level, forever. That is Endless mode.

---

## The arc at a glance

1. **Prelude** — village, celebration, crash, the king's reveal, minions. Outside: the king in a
   cage of light on the Star-Beast's back, going away over the hill, and glowing slimes left
   behind. Follow the king.
2. **Chapter 1: the Outskirts** — chase through the villages. The Star-Beast's escort. Boss. The
   king carried off into the fens.
3. **Chapter 2: the Drowned Fens** — the chase through the marsh. Boss. The king carried off up the
   mountain road.
4. **Chapter 3: the Mountain Road** — the climb. The Star-Beast's camp. Boss. The king dragged
   through the vault door.
5. **Chapter 4: the Vault** — the dungeon under the mountain. The king. The Dragon.
6. **After** — the deep dungeon as Endless.

Early Access ships Chapters 1 and 2. Each ends with the king carried off, which is a cliff-hanger
either way.

## Prelude

As written in `../PRELUDE_NOTES.md`. This draft's specifics:

- **The crawl** says: a far, far away land, a kingdom ringed by villages, a king descended from
  the first king who sealed a dragon under the mountain long ago; the Knight Squad rides out
  whenever a village calls; you are its newest knights.
- **The great hall** has a tapestry the player walks past during the celebration: the first king,
  a dragon, a door in a mountain. Speeding through: there is a dragon in a mountain somewhere.
- **The crashed ship** is a jagged black thing half-buried in the courtyard, glowing in its
  cracks. Nothing inside it explains anything; it is a star-beast's shell as far as the land is
  concerned.
- **Outside:** the fight is over. The king is inside a cage of glowing light, slung on the
  Star-Beast's back, going away over the hill. He is punching the bars. Glowing slimes are
  spilling out of the wake it leaves. Everyone follows the king.

## Chapter 1 — The Outskirts

**Region:** farmland, orchards, the villages. **Climate:** neutral: the teaching chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** the Star-Beast's wake, a road of glowing footprints with every
creature near it swollen and lit. Ahead on the road, the cage bobbing away.

**The road:** the land's ordinary creatures: slimes as grunts, thrown-turnip scarecrow-folk as
ranged, will-o'-wisps as casters, a boar as brute. Plain early; aura-lit along the wake.

**The mid-chapter beat:** a village square where the Star-Beast stopped to rest. The cage sits in
the middle of it, the king inside, glaring, and the Star-Beast is off-screen. The player fights
across the square toward the cage. The Star-Beast's hand comes in from the side and takes it before
the player reaches it. Speeding through: the king is in the cage; you almost got him.

**The boss:** the Wake-Hound, the Star-Beast's first pet, a village dog that followed the glowing
road and grew to the size of a barn. It pounces along the lane (jump its landing) and whips its
tail across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a chestpiece, *Hound's Collar*, a heavy ring of
plate.

**Handoff:** the Hound falls. Over the next hill, the Star-Beast is wading into the fens, the cage
held high above the water, the king still punching the bars. The road runs down into the marsh.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Drowned Fens

**Region:** marshland, drowned ruins of an older castle, causeways. **Climate:** Earth
strengthened, Air weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the wake is a line of glowing water now. The ruins' inhabitants are
swelling up along it.

**The road:** fen creatures: frog-folk as grunts, blowpipe lizards as ranged, bog witches as
casters, a mud golem as brute. The Star-Beast's own minions from the prelude begin to appear in
the second half, escorting the cage.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the cage, sunk halfway into a bog on a causeway, the Star-Beast heaving
at it. The king inside is holding the bars shut on a frog-folk trying to get in. The player fights
along the causeway; the cage comes free and is carried off before they reach it. Speeding through:
almost got him again.

**The boss:** the Fen King, the drowned castle's old lord, a giant knight of mud and bone woken by
the wake and lit by it. It swings a rotten greatsword down the lane (jump the shockwave) and
sweeps a shield across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a sword, *Fenblade*, with an Earth
infusion.

**Handoff:** the Fen King sinks. Beyond the marsh the land rises into the mountain, and the wake
goes straight up it in a switchback of glowing footprints. High on the road, the cage. Higher
still, near the peak, a door in the rock, dark. The tapestry's door.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Mountain Road

**Region:** the mountain: switchbacks, cliffs, a snowline, the Star-Beast's camp at the top.
**Climate:** Ice strengthened, Fire weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the road up, lined with the Star-Beast's escort: every aura-lit thing
it collected on the way, standing guard.

**The road:** mountain creatures: ice wolves as grunts, spear-throwing yeti-folk as ranged, frost
sprites as casters, a stone ram as brute. And the escort: amped versions of Chapter 1 and 2's
creatures, the wake's collection.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the camp. The cage at its centre, the Star-Beast standing at the vault
door with one hand on it, the door not moving. It turns and looks at the cage. Speeding through:
it can't open the door; the king can.

**The boss:** the Camp Warden, the Star-Beast's champion: a brute from the prelude's minions, fed
on the wake until it is the size of a tower. It slams the ground along the lane (jump the
shockwave) and drags a glowing chain across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a helmet,
*Warden's Helm*.

**Handoff:** the Warden falls. The Star-Beast takes the king out of the cage, drags him to the
door, and presses his hand flat on it. The door glows in the shape of the first king's crest and
grinds open. It hauls him inside. The door begins to close. The player runs for it.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Vault

**Region:** the first king's dungeon under the mountain: halls of old stone, traps, chains, the
Dragon's chamber at the bottom. **Climate:** Fire strengthened, Ice weakened, so the Fire knight
gets the finale, and the dragon breathes fire.

**The road:** the vault's own guardians, set by the first king a thousand years ago and woken by
the door: armoured skeleton knights as grunts, crossbow gargoyles as ranged, dead wizards as
casters, an iron golem as brute. The Star-Beast's escort pours in behind the player. The two
sides fight each other as much as the player.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king. At the bottom of the last stair, against the wall, badly
hurt, the cage broken beside him. Alive. Beyond him, the chamber: a mountain of gold, and on it,
the Dragon, asleep, with the Star-Beast climbing its neck. The king points at it. Speeding through:
the king is hurt, and the alien is about to ride a dragon.

**The final boss, phase one:** the Star-Beast riding the Dragon. The Dragon sweeps its tail along
the lane (jump it) and breathes fire down one depth (change lane), while the Star-Beast throws
its glowing light from the saddle. When the Dragon is hurt enough it wakes fully, rears, and
throws the Star-Beast off.

**The final boss, phase two:** the Star-Beast alone, glowing, furious, in the gold. It stamps a
ring of light across the floor (jump it) and fires a beam down one depth (change lane). The
Dragon watches from the hoard and does not help either side. **Drop:** a pet, *Hatchling*, a small
dragon that follows the player; the Dragon itself is story.

**The ending:** the Star-Beast bursts. The Dragon looks at the player for a long moment, then
curls back up on its gold and goes to sleep. The player walks back up the stair past the king,
who has got to his feet.

## After

The results screen. Then the throne room: the king bandaged on his throne, the cage's broken bars
mounted above it as a trophy, the vault's tapestry with a small new figure sewn into it.

**Endless:** the Dragon's chamber was not the bottom. The first king's dungeon goes down further
than anyone has mapped, and everything in it woke up when the door opened. The Knight Squad goes
down, level by level, forever. That is Endless mode.

## Speeding through

Village, castle, crash, buff king, fight outside. The king lost. He is in a cage on the monster's
back, going that way. Chase it. Every time you nearly reach the cage it gets away. Through the
fens, up the mountain, to a door. The monster makes the king open it. Inside: the king hurt, a
dragon, the monster riding it. Beat it. The dragon goes back to sleep. Take the king home.
