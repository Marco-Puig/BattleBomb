# Candidate 7 — A Crown Too Many

**Pitch:** the alien buys an army by handing out cheap tin crowns and titles to the kingdom's
greediest monsters, while he hunts the real crown: the one that commands an army of ancient stone
knights. The heroes topple a kingdom of terrible little kings and crash his coronation.

**Tone:** a medieval power struggle played as royal farce. Every petty monster thinks this is
finally its chance to become important. The alien is a cheat who treats every promise as
disposable, and that is what beats him.

**Working names:** the artifact is the First Crown. The alien is the Commander. His messenger is
the Herald.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is the First Crown, kept beneath an old royal abbey. It commands the kingdom's
  ancient stone guardians, the ones standing as monuments in every courtyard. It does not need a
  rightful heir: whoever wears it can give the order.
- **Why the aliens want it:** a stone army. The Commander promises land and titles to local
  creatures in exchange for help finding it, and his buffs make the promises credible. He keeps
  the stone army for himself.
- **The king** pursued the Commander from the castle and reached the vault first. The Commander
  followed and beat him, but the king collapsed across the vault's narrow entrance and blocked
  it. He is found badly wounded beside the crown, and lives. On the way out, an ambush: the
  Commander takes the crown and abandons his allies on the spot.
- **Where it ends:** the old coronation city, in the throne hall, mid-coronation.
- **Final boss:** the Commander, crowned, with his own strength and the crown's stone servants.
- **Endless:** the tin crowns. The Commander stamped out thousands, and every monster that finds
  one in a ditch decides it is a baron. The Knight Squad puts down pretenders at the crash site
  forever.

---

## The arc at a glance

1. **Prelude** — village, celebration, crash, the king's reveal, minions. Before the attack, a
   courtyard monument: stone guardians bowing to a crowned ruler. A pigeon nests in his beard.
2. **Chapter 1: the Village** — His Majesty the village slime. The Herald flees with the king's

3. **Chapter 2: the Forest Toll Road** — the goblin duke. Boss.
4. **Chapter 3: the Marsh Castle** — the toad duchess. Boss.

## Prelude

As written in `../PRELUDE_NOTES.md`. This draft's specifics:

- **Before the attack,** the knights pass a courtyard monument: stone guardians bowing to an
  ancient crowned ruler. A pigeon is nesting in the ruler's beard. It looks like ordinary castle
  decoration. Speeding through: statues, a crown, a pigeon.
- **The crashed ship:** an alien projection displays a crown matching the courtyard statue.
  Nearby, minions load cheap metal crowns onto a cart. Speeding through: they want a crown, and
  they've brought fakes.
- **Outside:** the king and the Commander are gone. The glowing slimes remain.

## Chapter 1 — The Village

**Region:** the tutorial village. **Climate:** neutral: the teaching chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** an enormous buffed slime wearing a tin crown, sitting on the village
well as though it were a throne. Its first act as baron was to eat its own tax collector.

**The road:** the land's ordinary creatures: slimes as grunts, thrown-turnip scarecrows as
ranged, will-o'-wisps as casters, a boar as brute. The crowned ones glow.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the Herald, an alien in a tabard, nailing a pictorial proclamation to
the well: a crown, a monster, a plot of land. Speeding through: the alien is handing out
kingdoms.

**The boss:** His Majesty the Village Slime, crowned, glowing, on the well. It bounces along the
lane and lands (jump the splash) and rolls across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a helmet,
*Tin Crown*, which is exactly what it sounds like.

**Handoff:** the slime bursts and the village road reopens. The Herald rolls up his proclamation
and hurries off up the forest road toward the next newly appointed ruler. The king's torn banner
is tied to his cart as a trophy. Follow the Herald.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Forest Toll Road

**Region:** a forest road with a toll booth every hundred paces. **Climate:** Earth strengthened,
Air weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** toll booths, and the goblin duke's glowing followers manning them.
Travellers are being charged separately for each boot.

**The road:** forest bandits and creatures: goblins as grunts, sling-goblins as ranged, a goblin
hedge-wizard as caster, a chained forest bear as brute. Ordinary bandits on the approaches;
glowing followers around the duke.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the Herald's cart at a booth, being charged a toll by the duke's own
goblins. Speeding through: even the alien's messenger has to pay.

**The boss:** the Goblin Duke, on a throne built from confiscated boots, glowing, wielding a
toll-gate as a club. He swings the gate down the lane (jump it) and drops the barrier across one
depth (change lane). **Drop:** a pair of boots, *Both Boots*, sold as a pair for once.

**Handoff:** the duke falls. His followers strip the booths. Down the road the Herald's cart is
splashing into the marsh, and past the treeline a castle stands in the water with a flood pouring
out of its front door.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Marsh Castle

**Region:** a drowned castle in the fens. **Climate:** Ice strengthened, Fire weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the toad duchess has flooded her own banquet hall and is holding
court in it anyway. Her followers glow in the water.

**The road:** marsh creatures: frog-folk as grunts, blowpipe lizards as ranged, bog witches as
casters, a mud golem as brute.

**The mid-chapter beat:** a great hall wall painted with the proclamation, larger: find the crown,
give it to the alien, receive a kingdom. Beneath the throne in the picture, a stone army,
painted small. Speeding through: the alien keeps the army.

**The boss:** the Toad Duchess, in her flooded hall, on a floating throne. She belly-flops along
the lane (jump the wave) and lashes her tongue across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a
chestpiece, *Duchess's Corset*.

**Handoff:** the duchess sinks. The flood drains out the front door and the Herald's cart, stuck
in the mud, is pulled free by his goblins and driven off toward a mountain quarry where something
enormous is sitting down.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Quarry and the Abbey

**Region:** a mountain quarry, then the abbey road, then the vault beneath the abbey.
**Climate:** Air strengthened, Earth weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the ogre lord, who wears a throne strapped to his backside so he
never has to stop sitting in authority, directing his glowing followers to carve his likeness
into the quarry wall. Fresh golden sword-cuts on the quarry gate: the king got here first.

**The road:** quarry creatures: kobold miners as grunts, rock-throwing hill trolls as ranged, a
dust wizard as caster, a rolling stone-ball beetle as brute. Then the abbey road, and the abbey's
crypt.

**The boss (mid-chapter):** the Ogre Lord, throne and all. He sits down along the lane and the
ground shakes (jump the shockwave) and swings his sceptre across one depth (change lane).
**Drop:** a sword, *Sceptre*, a mace that counts as a sword.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king. In the abbey vault beside the First Crown, badly wounded,
collapsed across the narrow entrance so the Commander's soldiers cannot get through. He points to
a carving on the wall: the same image as the courtyard monument. The crown commands the stone
guardians. Speeding through: the king held the door; the crown wakes the statues.

**Handoff:** the knights drive the Commander's guard back and carry the king and the crown out.
At the abbey gate the Herald returns with the remaining monster nobles for an ambush. It ends
with the Commander taking the crown himself. He abandons his allies immediately, walks off down
the road toward the old coronation city, and seals its gate behind him, with his nobles outside
along with everyone else.

---

## Chapter 5 — The Coronation City

**Region:** the old coronation city, occupied, its throne hall. **Climate:** neutral: the finale
is fair to all four.

**What you see on arrival:** stone guardians rising along the road, each displaying the
Commander's new banner. The squabbling bullies have become a real threat.

**The road:** stone guardians in every archetype: statue knights as grunts, gargoyles as ranged,
statue priests as casters, a colossus as brute. Some former monster nobles are still hostile;
others are busy tearing down portraits of the ruler who cheated them. The goblin duke opens a
barricade for the knights, then tries to charge the departing alien soldiers an exit toll.

**The final boss:** the Commander, crowned, in the throne hall, surrounded by stone courtiers who
applaud whenever he gestures at them. Outside, ranks of guardians assemble to march. He stamps
and a wave of guardians rises along the floor (jump it), and he sends a stone courtier sliding
across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a piece of equipment, *Courtier's Fanfare*, an active
that stuns nearby enemies; the First Crown is story.

**The ending:** the crown is knocked from his head and the stone soldiers freeze mid-march. Behind
the empty throne, two stone courtiers remain posed in enthusiastic applause. The surviving alien
soldiers run.

## After

The results screen. Then the abbey: the recovered king orders the First Crown dismantled. It could
make him the most powerful monarch the kingdom has ever had. After seeing it in someone else's
hands he would rather have capable knights. The guardians settle back into harmless monuments.
The villages remove the toll booths. At the celebration a captured heap of counterfeit crowns is
piled in the square; a village child puts one on an ordinary gray slime, which sits on a bread
roll looking pleased with its modest domain. The knights exchange a look.

**Endless:** the Commander stamped out thousands of tin crowns, and every monster that finds one
in a ditch decides it is a baron. The Knight Squad puts down pretenders at the crash site,
forever. That is Endless mode.

## Speeding through

Village, castle, crash, buff king, fight outside. The alien is handing out crowns and every monster
with one thinks it's a king. Beat the slime king, the goblin duke, the toad duchess, the ogre
lord. Find the real crown and the king hurt beside it. The alien grabs the crown and the statues
wake up. Chase him to his coronation. Knock the crown off. The statues stop.
