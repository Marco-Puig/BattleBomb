# Candidate 8 — The Last Banquet

**Pitch:** the aliens are stealing food. All of it. They brew it into a glowing drink that makes
monsters huge, and they want the one cooking pot that never runs empty so they can feed an army
forever. The knights follow the stolen food to a monstrous royal banquet and crash it.

**Tone:** the most openly absurd of the five, with real stakes under the nonsense: a funny army,
but it is eating everyone else's winter. The king guards the last tiny pastry on an absurdly
large plate.

**Working names:** the artifact is the Everfull Cauldron. The alien is the Commander.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is the Everfull Cauldron, a battered three-legged pot that makes an endless
  supply of whatever is first cooked inside it, until it is tipped completely empty. It looks
  less valuable than almost anything the knights have looted.
- **Why the aliens want it:** the Commander's brew empowers monsters, and he recruits by promising
  unlimited food and supernatural strength. He only has enough brew for scattered bands. With the
  cauldron, he can feed a whole invading host.
- **The king** followed the Commander to the old harvest hall and fought to stop him filling
  barrels from the cauldron. He lost, but his last act was to shove the pot over and stop the
  flow. He is found badly injured beside the overturned cauldron, his golden sword broken, and
  lives. The Commander left with the barrels already filled.
- **Where it ends:** the ruined summer palace, at the banquet.
- **Final boss:** the Commander, who drinks the last concentrated serving himself.
- **Endless:** the cellars. The summer palace's cellars are stacked with brew barrels nobody
  found, and every creature that gets a drink is a new problem. The Knight Squad goes down
  breaking barrels forever.

---

- **Outside:** the king and the Commander are gone. The glowing slimes are menacing the village.

## Chapter 1 — The Village

**Region:** the tutorial village and farmland. **Climate:** neutral: the teaching chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** the invaders have taken the grain, the livestock, the wagons and the
entire bakery door. Stolen food is being fed into vats of glowing brew. Ordinary monsters drink
and swell.

**The road:** the land's ordinary creatures: slimes as grunts, thrown-turnip scarecrows as
ranged, will-o'-wisps as casters, a boar as brute. Plain first; brew-fed glowing ones around the
vats.

**The mid-chapter beat:** a slime drinking from a vat in the background, growing as it drinks.
Speeding through: the glowing drink makes them big.

**The boss:** the Mill Beast, a swollen farm creature fed from the biggest vat, blocking the mill.
It charges the lane (jump its lowered head) and swings a millstone across one depth (change lane).
**Drop:** a pair of boots, *Bakery Door*, which are two halves of a door.

**Handoff:** the Mill Beast falls, the villagers are freed, and a food convoy is pulling out on
the orchard road. Beside the road: scraps of the king's cape and deep golden sword-cuts in a
wagon's side. The food, the king and the pot are all going the same way.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Orchards

**Region:** orchard country, cider barns, a boar camp. **Climate:** Earth strengthened, Air
weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the orchards stripped, the barns emptied, alien cooks tending
oversized vats with local monsters queuing to drink.

**The road:** orchard creatures: wasp-folk as grunts, apple-lobbing tree sprites as ranged, a
cider witch as caster, a boar as brute. The empty larders of every farmhouse keep the stakes
clear.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the convoy, halted at a barn while the cooks refill. The knights fight
toward it and it pulls away. Speeding through: nearly caught the food.

**The boss:** the Boar Warlord, wearing a bib, the biggest recruit in the orchards. It charges
along the lane (jump it) and flings a vat across one depth (change lane), leaving glowing brew on
the ground. **Drop:** a chestpiece, *The Bib*.

**Handoff:** the Warlord falls. Its recruits scatter. The convoy road drops into the marsh, where
a fishery's smoke is rising and something very large is being carried like a battering ram.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Marsh Fishery

**Region:** fens, a drowned fishery, smokehouses. **Climate:** Ice strengthened, Fire weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** marsh ogres carrying a fish the size of a boat as a battering ram,
smashing the smokehouses open for their stock.

**The road:** marsh creatures: frog-folk as grunts, blowpipe lizards as ranged, bog witches as
casters, a marsh ogre as brute.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the fishery's larder, empty, its door hanging off. A family of
frog-folk is sitting in it looking at the shelves. Speeding through: they stole everyone's
winter.

**The boss:** the Ogre Brothers, two marsh ogres carrying the fish between them. They swing it
down the lane (jump it) and sweep it across one depth (change lane); when one falls, the other
picks the fish up alone and gets slower. **Drop:** a sword, *The Fish*, a fish that counts as a
sword.

**Handoff:** the brothers fall and the fish is returned to the fishery. The convoy is climbing
toward a monastery on terraced gardens, and past it, an old harvest hall with its doors open and
light pouring out of them.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Monastery and the Harvest Hall

**Region:** a monastery's terraced gardens and granary, then the old harvest hall. **Climate:**
Air strengthened, Earth weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the terraces trampled, the granary broken open, and the monastery
pest, a rat that ate enough enchanted grain to outgrow the granary, sitting in the wreckage.

**The road:** monastery creatures: rat-folk as grunts, seed-spitting crows as ranged, a gardener
monk turned by the brew as caster, the granary pest as the brute. In the kitchen, a simple mural:
a three-legged pot pouring and pouring, until it is tipped over and stops. Speeding through: the
pot never runs out until you knock it over.

**The boss (mid-chapter):** the Granary Pest, a rat the size of the granary it outgrew. It leaps
along the lane (jump the landing) and whips its tail across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a
helmet, *Granary Lid*.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king. In the harvest hall, badly injured beside the overturned
cauldron, his golden sword broken nearby. He got here after the Commander, fought to stop the
barrels being filled, lost, and shoved the pot over with his last strength. The flow has stopped.
Speeding through: the king knocked the pot over and got hurt doing it.

**Handoff:** the knights beat the Commander's guards and recover the king and the cauldron. That
is a real win: the alien cannot feed an army forever now. But outside, the last loaded wagons are
rolling away toward the ruined summer palace, and crowds of hungry recruits are following
enormous painted banquet signs the same way. The brew already stolen is enough for tonight.

---

## Chapter 5 — The Summer Palace

**Region:** the banquet's supply line: wagon yards, smokehouses, palace kitchens, the great dining
hall. The palace glows the colour of the brew. **Climate:** Fire strengthened, Ice weakened, the
kitchens are roaring.

**The road:** the kingdom's worst creatures dressed for dinner with almost no understanding of
clothing: an ogre with a tablecloth through its belt, goblins duelling over the seat nearest the
serving vat. Every family from the journey, in its strongest form, waiting to be fed. The knights
smash barrels as they go.

**The final boss:** the Commander, in the king's stolen ceremonial chair at the head of the hall.
When the knights overturn the great serving vat before the host can drink, he grabs the last
concentrated serving and drinks it himself: the biggest, strongest version of the enemy who beat
the king. He slams the table along the lane (jump the plates) and hurls the serving vat's contents
across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a pet, *Table Slime*, a small gray slime with a bowl;
the cauldron is story.

**The ending:** the Commander falls. His unfed recruits scatter.

---

## After

The results screen. Then home: the cauldron cleaned, the king on his feet, contributing the first
ingredients to a stew. The villages share a feast that cannot run out. The knights finally sit
down to eat. Their bowls are enormous. The king has quietly taken a bowl twice as large. In the
last image an ordinary gray slime sneaks toward the cauldron, and the baker wordlessly places a
small bowl in its path. For once, the problem is solved without a sword.

**Endless:** the summer palace's cellars are stacked with barrels nobody found, and every creature
that gets a drink is a new problem. The Knight Squad goes down breaking barrels, forever. That
is Endless mode.

---

## Speeding through

Village, castle, crash, buff king, fight outside. Aliens are stealing all the food and brewing it
into a drink that makes monsters huge. Follow the food wagons. Beat the boar in a bib, the ogres
with a fish, the giant rat. Find the magic pot knocked over and the king hurt beside it. The
alien already has enough drink for one big party. Crash the party. Beat him. Everyone eats.
