# Candidate 9 — The Castle That Walked Away

**Pitch:** the alien lost his ship in the crash and needs a new stronghold. There is an old ruined
castle on a hill that is really a sleeping stone giant with a castle on its back, and one stone
wakes it. The knights rescue the king, and then have to catch a castle that is walking toward
home.

**Tone:** a pursuit adventure with one huge fairy-tale spectacle. The destination becomes a
monster. Goblins build a ladder that stops just below the window and a brute adds another goblin
to the top. This is the candidate that asks the most of environmental staging.

**Working names:** the artifact is the Marchheart. The alien is the Commander.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is the Marchheart, a small carved stone: the waking stone of an ancient
  fortress that walked into battle centuries ago. What looks like a ruined castle on a hill is a
  dormant stone giant carrying a castle on its back. The stone was removed long ago and kept in a
  chapel inside its walls.
- **Why the aliens want it:** the crash wrecked the ship's engine. The Commander landed an army
  and lost the machine meant to carry it. A walking fortress is a replacement.
- **The king** followed the Commander to the chapel, reached the Marchheart first, and was beaten
  defending it. He is found badly injured beside it, and lives. While the knights get him out, the
  Commander tears the stone from its mounting, carries it below, and the castle stands up.
- **Where it ends:** aboard the walking fortress, in its heart chamber, as it marches on the
  lowlands.
- **Final boss:** the Commander, drawing strength from the Marchheart while the fortress moves.
- **Endless:** the fortress kneels at the edge of the lowlands and becomes an outpost, but its
  lower halls are still full of the Commander's court and everything the giant's steps shook
  loose. The Knight Squad clears the kneeling giant level by level, forever.

---

## The arc at a glance

1. **Prelude** — village, celebration, crash, the king's reveal, minions. The king rests a boot
   on a stool that immediately collapses. Nobody is surprised.
2. **Chapter 1: the Village** — work gangs hauling chains and timber toward the hills. The king's
   sword-cuts on the same road.
3. **Chapter 2: the Logging Camps** — the forest work camps. Boss.
4. **Chapter 3: the Gorge** — the relief of the walking fortress. Boss.
5. **Chapter 4: the Old Fortress** — its overgrown grounds, the chapel, the king. The castle
   stands up.
6. **Chapter 5: the Aqueduct and the Fortress** — catch a castle.
7. **After** — a king without a castle. Endless.

## Prelude

As written in `../PRELUDE_NOTES.md`. This draft's specifics:

- **The celebration:** the king rests a boot on a stool that immediately collapses. Nobody seems
  surprised.
- **The crashed ship:** the ruined engine, hanging out of the hull. Alien minions compare it with
  an image of a small carved stone, and beside it a crude picture of a castle standing on
  enormous legs. Speeding through: their engine is broken, and they want a castle with legs.
- **Outside:** the king and the Commander are gone. The glowing slimes remain.

## Chapter 1 — The Village

**Region:** the tutorial village and its road into the hills. **Climate:** neutral: the teaching
chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** buffed monsters hauling chains, timber and stolen ironwork toward
the hills in long lines.

**The road:** the land's ordinary creatures: slimes as grunts, thrown-turnip scarecrows as
ranged, will-o'-wisps as casters, a boar as brute. The hauling gangs glow.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the village smithy stripped to the anvil, an alien overseer measuring a
doorframe with a ruler, deciding it is too small, and taking the door anyway. Speeding through:
they are collecting building materials.

**The boss:** the Gang Master, a village ox grown huge on the alien's aura, dragging a sled of
chains. It charges the lane (jump its lowered head) and swings the chain sled across one depth
(change lane). **Drop:** a chestpiece, *Chainmail*, made of actual chain.

**Handoff:** the Gang Master falls and its haul spills across the road. The remaining gangs head
for the forest, and along the same road, fresh golden sword-cuts in the trees. The king is
following the same building supplies.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Logging Camps

**Region:** forest logging camps. **Climate:** Earth strengthened, Air weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** whole stands of trees felled and stacked, glowing brutes carrying
logs three at a time, an alien overseer with a ledger.

**The road:** woodland creatures: beetle-folk as grunts, acorn-throwing squirrel-folk as ranged,
moss shamans as casters, a walking stump as brute. Ordinary in the woods; glowing in the camps.

**The mid-chapter beat:** goblins building a ladder that stops just below a watchtower window. A
brute adds another goblin to the top. Speeding through: this army is not very good at building.

**The boss:** the Foreman, a forest brute with a felled tree for a club. It brings the tree down
along the lane (jump the trunk) and sweeps it across one depth (change lane). **Drop:** a sword,
*Splinter*, a very large splinter.

**Handoff:** the Foreman falls. The log road runs out of the forest and down into a stony river
gorge, and on the gorge wall, an ancient carved relief is visible from the forest edge: a castle
walking.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Gorge

**Region:** a stony river gorge. **Climate:** Ice strengthened, Fire weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the relief, up close: a fortress on a giant's back striding into
battle, centuries ago, and beside it the small carved stone in the giant's chest. Speeding
through: the castle on the hill has legs, and that stone wakes it.

**The road:** gorge creatures: rock lizards as grunts, stone-throwing hill trolls as ranged, river
sprites as casters, a boulder crab as brute. The work gangs are hauling everything up the far
side toward an overgrown hill with a ruin on it.

**The mid-chapter beat:** an alien overseer measuring a gigantic footprint in the riverbed with a
ruler. It considers the result, and gets a second ruler. Speeding through: the giant has walked
here before.

**The boss:** the Gorge Troll, the biggest of the hill trolls, glowing, blocking the ford. It
slams a boulder down the lane (jump the shockwave) and rolls one across a single depth (change
lane). **Drop:** a helmet, *Second Ruler*, an alien overseer's helm.

**Handoff:** the troll falls. Up the far bank, the overgrown grounds of the abandoned royal
fortress, its hill shaped, from here, exactly like something crouching.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Old Fortress

**Region:** the fortress's overgrown grounds, its walls, and the chapel inside. **Climate:** Air
strengthened, Earth weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the work gangs are inside the walls, preparing. Gardens, sheds and
a goat have grown over the whole hill in the centuries it has knelt here.

**The road:** the fortress's own overgrowth: bramble sprites as grunts, thorn-shooters as ranged,
a hedge wizard as caster, an ivy-wrapped statue as brute. And the Commander's soldiers, everywhere.

**The mid-chapter beat:** the king. In the chapel, badly injured beside the Marchheart on its
mounting. He reached it before the Commander and was beaten defending it. The Commander has
withdrawn below to ready the empty heart chamber while his guards finish the job. Speeding
through: the king got here first and lost; the stone is still here.

**The boss:** the Chapel Guard, the Commander's biggest soldier, left to finish. It stamps the
chapel floor along the lane (jump the shockwave) and sweeps a glowing glaive across one depth
(change lane). **Drop:** a pair of boots, *Groundstompers*.

**Handoff:** the knights begin getting the king out and the Commander intercepts them at the
chapel steps. With the king unable to fight, the knights hold his troops while he tears the
Marchheart from its mounting and carries it below. They get the king past the outer wall just as
the ground rises. The castle stands up. Ancient legs unfold from the hillside, spilling gardens,
sheds and one extremely offended goat. Its first step crushes the road. Its next turns toward the
knights' home.

---

## Chapter 5 — The Aqueduct and the Fortress

**Region:** a damaged aqueduct running to a mountain bottleneck, then the fortress itself: outer
wall, courtyard, armoury, royal hall, the heart chamber. **Climate:** neutral: the finale is
fair to all four.

**What you see on arrival:** the injured king left at a refuge. The fortress, walking, visible
across the whole landscape, its footprints and the monsters it displaces marking the way. The
aqueduct meets the height of its battlements at the bottleneck: the way aboard.

**The road:** along the aqueduct, every creature the giant's steps have shaken loose. Aboard,
the Commander's soldiers have replaced a ruined medieval court with a much worse one. A servant
continues dusting a portrait while the wall tilts. The floor tilts with every step: the whole
chapter leans.

**The final boss:** the Commander, in the heart chamber, drawing strength from the Marchheart
while the fortress moves, surrounded by the machinery of his newly acquired throne. He stamps a
wave along the tilting floor (jump it) and swings a length of the giant's chain across one depth
(change lane). **Drop:** a piece of equipment, *Marchheart Shard*, an active that shakes the
ground; the Marchheart is story.

**The ending:** the Commander falls. The player pulls the Marchheart free with Light. The giant
stops at the edge of the lowlands and settles onto its knees instead of falling on the town below.

---

## After

The results screen. Then home: the king recovered, the stone returned to its chapel and now kept
well away from the heart chamber, the old fortress a permanent outpost overlooking the road. A
village sign points toward it; the painted arrow has been crossed out and redrawn several times.
At the homecoming the king unveils a statue of the knights. It is securely fastened to the ground.
He gives it a suspicious kick to make sure.

**Endless:** the kneeling fortress is an outpost, but its lower halls are still full of the
Commander's court and everything the giant's steps shook loose. The Knight Squad clears it level
by level, forever. That is Endless mode.

---

## Speeding through

Village, castle, crash, buff king, fight outside. The alien's ship is wrecked and its monsters are
carrying chains and timber into the hills. Follow them. A carving shows a castle with legs. Find
the king hurt beside the stone that wakes it. The alien takes the stone and the castle stands up
and walks toward home. Run along the aqueduct, jump aboard, fight down to the heart, pull the
stone out. The castle kneels.
