# Candidate 10 — The Dragon in the Treasure Chest

**Pitch:** the aliens are treasure hunters after an ancient jewel wrapped in claw-shaped gold. The
jewel is a dragon egg, about to hatch, and the kingdom promised long ago to keep it safe. The
alien wants it as bait: with the egg he can draw the mother out from under her mountain and
harness her.

**Tone:** a treasure hunt that becomes a rescue, with the warmest payoff. Small nesting creatures
all through the journey set up the ending. The priceless artifact turns out to be a small,
inconvenient living thing.

**Working names:** the artifact is the Egg. The alien is the Commander.

---

## The answers this draft gives

- **The artifact** is a huge jewel wrapped in claw-shaped gold, kept in a mountain sanctuary. It is
  a dragon egg. Old carvings show a dragon placing it in human hands and a crowned figure carrying
  it into the sanctuary: the kingdom promised to keep it while its mother sleeps beneath the
  mountain.
- **Why the aliens want it:** the egg is bait. Their glowing harnesses drive creatures into a
  frenzy, amplify them and let the raiders steer them. With the egg, the Commander can draw the
  mother out and fit the biggest creature in the land with one.
- **The king** followed the Commander from the castle to protect the egg. The Commander beat him
  but could not carry the treasure through the sanctuary's narrow defences while the king still
  held the entrance. He is found badly injured beside it, and lives. On the way out, the
  Commander returns and steals the chest.
- **Where it ends:** an abandoned mountain monastery above hot springs, where the trap for the
  mother is built.
- **Final boss:** the Commander, wearing a spare harness himself, in a fortified hall too small for
  the dragon to enter.
- **Endless:** the harness workshops. The raiders built more harnesses than they used, and
  creatures keep turning up wearing them. The Knight Squad hunts harnessed beasts at the crash
  site, forever.

## Chapter 1 — The Village

**Region:** the tutorial village. **Climate:** neutral: the teaching chapter.

**What you see on arrival:** some ordinary creatures fleeing the glowing ones. A gray slime
squeezes through a fence while its empowered twin ploughs through the boards.

**The road:** the land's ordinary creatures: slimes as grunts, thrown-turnip scarecrows as
ranged, will-o'-wisps as casters, a boar as brute. The knights still fight dangerous locals, but
the invaders are clearly making the whole land more violent.

**The mid-chapter beat:** raiders loading a padded chest onto a wagon. It is empty. Speeding
through: they haven't found it yet.

**The boss:** the Village Beast, the biggest buffed creature in the village, a bull in a glowing
harness. It charges the lane (jump its lowered head) and swings its horns across one depth
(change lane). **Drop:** a pair of boots, *Fence-Jumpers*.

**Handoff:** the beast falls. On an alien treasure wagon beside it: golden sword-cuts. The raiders
are heading for old shrines in the forest, and so did the king.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Bright Forest

**Region:** a vivid forest and its old shrines. **Climate:** Earth strengthened, Air weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** creatures fitted with glowing alien harnesses, driven into a frenzy
and used to break open shrines. Each shrine holds a carving of the same jewel beneath a dragon's
folded wing.

**The road:** forest creatures: beetle-folk as grunts, acorn-throwing squirrel-folk as ranged,
moss shamans as casters, a woodland brute. Natural inhabitants first; harnessed ones around the
raiders and their captains.

**The mid-chapter beat:** a woodland brute sheltering its tiny offspring under a cooking pot as
the fight passes. Speeding through: the monsters have babies too.

**The boss:** the Shrine-Breaker, a harnessed forest bear the raiders use as a battering ram. It
charges the lane (jump it) and is whipped by its handler into a sweep across one depth (change
lane); killing the handler first slows it. **Drop:** a chestpiece, *Broken Harness*.

**Handoff:** the bear falls and its harness sparks out. The raiders' road runs down into marsh
ruins, and on the last shrine's carving the picture is clearer: a dragon placing the jewel in
human hands.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Marsh Ruins and the Foothills

**Region:** marsh ruins, then frost-covered foothills, then the mountain sanctuary. **Climate:**
Ice strengthened, Fire weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** the ruins' carvings, clearer still: a crowned figure carrying the
jewel into a mountain sanctuary. Speeding through: the kingdom promised to keep it safe.

**The road:** marsh creatures: frog-folk as grunts, blowpipe lizards as ranged, bog witches as
casters, a mud golem as brute. Then foothill creatures: ice wolves as grunts, yeti-folk as
ranged, frost sprites as casters, a stone ram as brute. Harnessed ones around the raiding
captains.

**The mid-chapter beat:** a marsh creature attacks anyone who approaches its nest, then returns to
adjusting a ludicrously small blanket. Later, beyond the last raiding captain's camp, the broken
tip of the king's golden sword. Speeding through: the king is ahead and in trouble.

**The boss:** the Raiding Captain, the Commander's lieutenant, riding a harnessed frost ram. The
ram charges the lane (jump it) and the captain throws a net across one depth (change lane).
**Drop:** a bow, *Captain's Crossbow*.

**Handoff:** the captain falls. The sanctuary is beyond. The king is inside, badly injured, holding
its narrow entrance, and the jewel is behind him. The knights drive off the Commander's guards and
lift the jewel into an open chest. It rocks. A tiny claw punches through its surface and
withdraws. The sanctuary wall shows the rest: the mother sleeps beneath the mountain. As the
knights bring the king out, the Commander returns with his raiders and steals the chest. The
egg's movement has told him there is no time to wait. He heads straight up the mountain.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Hot Springs and the Monastery

**Region:** hot springs, then an abandoned mountain monastery, its chain stations, its courtyard,
its fortified hall. **Climate:** Fire strengthened, Ice weakened.

**What you see on arrival:** enormous chains, broken cages, scorched alien equipment: parts of one
visible operation. The Commander is building a trap big enough for a dragon.

**The road:** the raiders' whole force, and the strongest harnessed beasts of every family met on
the journey, manning the chain stations.

**The mid-chapter beat:** at the summit, the Commander opens the chest. The egg's cries wake its
mother. She rises from the mountain and reaches for it, and is caught by the prepared chains and a
glowing harness. The knights fight through the chain stations and release her, one station at a
time: the reverse of everything the aliens have done all game. She tears away the last of the
harness and destroys the army in the courtyard. Speeding through: they trapped the dragon; you
let her go; she wrecked them.

**The final boss:** the Commander, retreated with the egg into a fortified hall too small for the
mother to enter, wearing a spare harness himself. He has lost his army and his dragon but is
still the enemy who beat the king. He charges the lane in the harness (jump it) and fires the
harness's glow across one depth (change lane). Late in the fight the hall's roof begins to glow
red from the outside. **Drop:** a pet, *Hatchling*, the small dragon itself, once the story has
handed it back; a helmet, *Commander's Harness*, for the rack.

**The ending:** the knights recover the chest and carry it outside. The egg hatches before they
reach the mother. A tiny dragon crawls out, bites a knight's glove and sneezes a spark onto the
velvet. The knights hold it up. The mother lowers her head to them, repeating the gesture from the
sanctuary's oldest carving.

## After

The results screen. Then home: the recovering king honours the knights. During the celebration the
hatchling lands on the banquet table and starts eating the commemorative cake. Outside, its mother
has carefully folded herself around the castle without fitting in the courtyard at all.

**Endless:** the raiders built more harnesses than they used, and creatures keep turning up wearing
them. The Knight Squad hunts harnessed beasts at the crash site, forever. That is Endless mode.

## Speeding through

Village, castle, crash, buff king, fight outside. Aliens with empty treasure chests are looking
for a jewel. Follow them. Every carving shows the jewel under a dragon's wing. Find the king hurt
guarding it. The jewel kicks: it's an egg. The alien steals it and uses it to trap the mother.
Free her. Beat him. Give the baby back.
